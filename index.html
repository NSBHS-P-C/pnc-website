Welcome to the NSBHS P&C

The North Sydney Boys High School Parents & Citizens Association brings parents, carers, staff and the wider community together to support our boys' learning and wellbeing. Led by a committee of volunteer parents, the P&C oversees the school canteen and uniform shop, runs the Falcon Sports Club, and organises events and fundraising that benefit every student.

New to North Sydney Boys? A warm welcome to you and your son. The links below will help you with lunch orders, uniforms, sport and getting involved.
