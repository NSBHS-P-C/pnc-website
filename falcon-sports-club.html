Falcon Sports Club

Falcon Sports Club is a subcommittee of the NSBHS P&C, founded by a group of sports-loving parents. Running alongside the school's own sports program, it offers sessions before and after school and on weekends, so our boys can stay active, build skills and make friends beyond the classroom.

The club is managed by a dedicated team of volunteer parents, and families are warmly welcome to cheer, encourage and support along the way.

Our Sports

BadmintonCoached after-school sessions in the school gym, grouped from beginner to advanced.
BasketballTeams in the Northern Suburbs Basketball Association competition, with training at school.
OrienteeringMap-and-compass events around Sydney, with Saturday morning training each term.
SquashTraining twice a week at MAASH Sport & Fitness, Marsfield.
Table TennisWeekly coached sessions in the school gym for players of all levels.
TaekwondoClasses with Chung Do Taekwondo at a discounted rate for NSBHS students.
TennisProfessional coaching on the school tennis courts twice a week.
VolleyballWeekly coached training in the school gym, plus a self-training session.

For training times, venues, competitions, fees and each sport's coordinator, please see the Falcon Sport Handbook.

Rewards and events

Players can earn Falcon Sport certificates, participation badges and the Don Northey Award. Senior students can coach younger players through the Falcon Youth Coach Program, and Falcon sport counts towards the Duke of Edinburgh Award. Each Term 4, all players are invited to the end-of-year BBQ to meet teammates and players from other sports.

Contact

General enquiries: falcon@nsbhspnc.org. For questions about a particular sport, please contact its sports coordinator, listed in the Handbook.
