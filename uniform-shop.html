Uniform Shop

The P&C Uniform Shop supplies everything your son needs for school, from everyday uniform to sports wear and accessories.

WhereNext to Building C – follow the Uniform Shop signs
OpenMonday and Thursday, 8:15am – 1:30pm
PayCard only (no cash), or order online
New studentsBy appointment, from Orientation Day

Opening hours

Monday and Thursday, 8:15am – 1:30pm during school terms.

Extra opening hours are added in Term 4 and Term 1. These will be announced to families once they are set.

New students: please book an appointment

Once your son has accepted his offer, you are welcome to visit the Uniform Shop from Orientation Day onwards. This is our busiest time of year, so an appointment is required. We are unable to serve families without an appointment during this period.

Booking details will be added here. In the meantime, please email uniform@nsbhspnc.org with any questions.

Shop online

You can also order uniforms online through our Uniform Shop store.

Second-hand uniforms

Second-hand uniforms are run by the Student Representative Council (SRC), not the P&C. Please contact the school for details.

Volunteer in the Uniform Shop

The Uniform Shop is especially busy in Term 4 and Term 1, when new students are fitted for their uniforms. Volunteers are a great help at these times.

To offer your help, please email uniform@nsbhspnc.org.
