Events & Fundraising

The Events & Fundraising Subcommittee organises community events that bring families together, and fundraising that supports every student and the school. It is run entirely by parent volunteers.

Our Events

Crossroads

Date TBAWith the school

Former students return to share their journeys after school with our boys. The P&C supports this school event by providing the catering.

Falcon End-of-Year BBQ

Term 4Falcon Sports Club

Falcon players and families celebrate the year together, meeting teammates and players from other sports.

Staff Appreciation Lunch

Last day of Term 4P&C

On the last day of the school year, a big day at school when the year’s HSC results are celebrated, the P&C hosts a festive lunch to thank all school staff for everything they do for our boys. It is also a favourite day for our volunteers: many parents come back every year to help out and catch up with old friends.
