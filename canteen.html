Canteen

The NSBHS P&C runs the school canteen as a not-for-profit service for our students. We receive some foods from outside suppliers, but make most of our food right where it is served. Our aim is a clean environment and tasty recess and lunch, so the boys enjoy their breaks and study well.

OpenEvery school day at recess and lunch
WhereBuilding C – follow the Canteen signs
Pay at the counterCash, card or student card

Order lunch online

Lunch can be ordered ahead of time through Flexischools. Orders close at 8:00am on the day.

New families: please create a Flexischools account and add your son before placing your first order. Money in your Flexischools account can also be used with your son's student card at the counter.

ORDER ON FLEXISCHOOLS

Dietary needs

Our lunch menu includes Halal, vegetarian and gluten-free options. These are not available at recess or for Wednesday lunch. Dietary information is shown on each item when you order online.

Menu

Families can view our full menu and prices in Flexischools after creating an account.

Contact

Questions about the canteen? Email canteen@nsbhspnc.org.

How to find us

The canteen is in Building C at North Sydney Boys High School, 127 Falcon Street, Crows Nest. Once you are on campus, follow the Canteen signs.
